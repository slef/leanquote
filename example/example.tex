\section*{leandecl example: 3D translational tiling}

A \emph{prototile} is a finite list of points of $\mathbb{Z}^3$:
\leandecl{Abstract.leandecl}{Point, Prototile}

Two points are \emph{adjacent} if their $\ell^1$ distance is $1$, and a prototile is
\emph{connected} if any two of its points are joined by a path of adjacent points:
\leandecl{Abstract.leandecl}{Adjacent, Connected}

$P$ \emph{tiles} $\mathbb{Z}^3$ if some set $T$ of translations puts every point in exactly
one translate $t + P$. Here with its doc comment:
\leandecl[doc]{Abstract.leandecl}{Tiles}

\textbf{Theorem.} Tiling $\mathbb{Z}^3$ with a connected prototile is co-RE-complete,
and hence undecidable:
\leandecl{Abstract.leandecl}{tiling_coRE_complete, TranslationTiling.Abstract.tiling_undecidable}
